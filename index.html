Connect to your packing lists

Pick the folder that holds your project-manager folders. The app reads the file names, so you can search by job, shipper, date or project manager and open the packing list right here. Nothing is moved or renamed.

On a phone or any computer: click Sign in with Microsoft and use your work account. On a PC you can also pick the folder directly:

  1. Click Choose folder and pick that top folder.
  2. When Edge asks, allow it to view and edit files (the app only adds new packing lists you log, and its own receiving-data.json for locations and notes).
  3. Choose Allow on every visit so you don't have to pick it again.

Pick the packing-list folder

Open the folder that holds the project-manager folders, then click Use this folder. You only do this once on each phone or computer.

Folder shared with you by someone else? Paste its link instead

In OneDrive, open the folder, click Share > Copy link, and paste it here. (Or open it in OneDrive on the web and click Add shortcut to My files; then it shows in the list above.)
